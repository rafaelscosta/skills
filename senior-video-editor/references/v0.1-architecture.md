# Senior Video Editor v0.1 architecture

## Goal

Prove that an agentic editing system can turn an ambiguous video request and raw source material into senior-recognizable editorial decisions before expanding into the full finishing stack.

## Vertical slice

```text
VideoEditContract
  ↓
Footage Intelligence
  ↓
Story Edit
  ↓
Senior Video Editor integration
  ↓
Independent Supervising Video Editor
```

## Intentionally deferred specialists

The following remain explicit gaps in v0.1 and must not be simulated as fully certified competencies:

- timing-rhythm
- attention-choreography
- cinematic-continuity
- objective-driven-edit profiles
- motion-design
- sound-post
- color-finishing
- vfx-compositing
- captions-accessibility
- media-pipeline
- ai-assisted-post
- delivery-qc

## Promotion principle

A new specialist should become a standalone skill only when it has a distinct decision policy, inputs/outputs, failure modes, and eval surface. Platform or software differences should normally be profiles or adapters, not new cognitive skills.

## Success definition

The slice succeeds when blind reviewers prefer or accept its editorial outputs for the right reasons: stronger structure, selection, clarity, traceability, and diagnosis — not merely more text or more effects.
