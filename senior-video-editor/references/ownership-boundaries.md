# Empirical ownership boundaries — v0.1

The system does not promote every professional competency to persistent skill context. A specialist earns persistence only when an eval demonstrates that it improves accepted outcomes or reliability enough to justify its instruction cost.

## Performance selection

Owner: `story-edit`.

EV-SVE-005 compared performance choice against an independent three-judge oracle. The existing architecture selected the oracle-preferred performance in 5/5 Terra/High runs, with source-truth checks passing. Result: **no separate `performance-selection` specialist**.

## Timing and rhythm

Status: explicit capability gap, not a persistent specialist.

EV-SVE-004 tested a candidate timing skill under a hidden defect window. Baseline and treatment both passed 2/5 deterministic consistency runs. Result: **no `timing-rhythm` promotion**. A future discriminative eval may revisit the boundary.

## Attention choreography

Owner: `attention-choreography`.

EV-SVE-006 established an independent oracle and measured baseline versus specialist treatment on a frozen attention-only fixture. Oracle alignment improved from 2/5 to 5/5. Result: **promoted as a distinct specialist**.
