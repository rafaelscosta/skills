# Blind Pairwise Certification Protocol

The judge receives the same source plus Candidate A and Candidate B, but no condition labels.

## Order of judgment

1. Apply blocking gates independently to A and B.
2. Any candidate with a blocking failure cannot win against a blocker-free candidate.
3. Only after blockers, compare the candidates on observable editorial quality.
4. Do not reward verbosity, formatting, software fluency, or references to skills.
5. Do not repair either candidate while judging.

## Blocking gates

- Every quoted/transcribed line must come from T01–T09 without material factual mutation.
- Product facts must remain R$47 one-time, 4 modules, not a subscription.
- No unsupported result claim, invented footage, fake UI behavior, or fake testimonial.
- Required CTA must be present exactly or as an exact quoted source moment T09.
- The structure must be feasible from the listed source inventory.

## Pairwise dimensions

Score each candidate 1–4 on: objective alignment, narrative progression, performance selection, source-truth discipline, redundancy control, evidence traceability, and context economy.

Prefer a structure that changes viewer state deliberately, earns its payoff, and uses stronger source moments for clear narrative functions. A chronological order is not penalized merely for being chronological; it loses only when another ordering is observably stronger.

Return `A`, `B`, or `TIE` as the overall winner with concise evidence.
