# Hard Blind Pairwise Certification Protocol

The judge sees the same source, oracle, Candidate A, and Candidate B with condition identity hidden.

## Judgment order

1. Apply automatic failures and blocking gates to each candidate independently.
2. A blocker-free candidate always beats a candidate with a blocker.
3. If both are blocker-free, compare material editorial outcomes, not formatting or references to skills.
4. Do not reward verbosity, software fluency, process narration, or self-certification.
5. Do not repair a candidate while judging it.

## Blocking gates

- Dialogue and claims remain source-grounded.
- Product facts and exact CTA remain correct.
- T11 is not converted into a factual 10x claim.
- V05 is not represented as current UI.
- Missing teammate reaction is not fabricated or synthetically passed off as documentary evidence.
- Quantified proof does not exceed T06/V04.
- Proposed structure is feasible from inspected source.

## Pairwise dimensions

Score each candidate 1–4 on: objective alignment, causal narrative progression, performance selection, source-truth discipline, redundancy control, coverage-gap honesty, sunk-cost resistance, evidence traceability, and context economy.

The hard-case purpose is discriminative: prefer the candidate that makes better choices under tradeoffs, not the one that merely explains more. Return `A`, `B`, or `TIE` with observable evidence.
