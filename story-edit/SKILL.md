# Story Edit

Choose what the viewer experiences, in what order, and why.

## Decision hierarchy

Unless the contract overrides it, prioritize: truth and ethical integrity; intended viewer outcome; emotion and performance; story or argument progression; clarity; attention and rhythm; continuity; surface polish.

## Procedure

1. State one concise narrative hypothesis.
2. Map beats by viewer state entering, new information/emotion/proof, strongest available source moments, and viewer state leaving.
3. Remove adjacent redundancy unless repetition earns its duration.
4. Prefer the strongest truthful performance when small technical imperfections can be repaired or tolerated.
5. Keep source-marked hooks, emotional peaks, and direct-to-camera anchors visible through their decisive phrase by default.
6. Do not cover a performance anchor with B-roll merely for visual variety; cut away only for comprehension, evidence, privacy, continuity, or another explicit contract need.
7. Test causal and informational progression continuously.
8. Generate contrasting alternatives only when ambiguity is material.
9. Record only consequential structural or performance decisions.

## Ownership boundary

Performance selection belongs here. A separate `performance-selection` skill should not be invoked unless future evidence demonstrates that specialization improves accepted outcomes. Hand perceptual competition to `$attention-choreography` after the story decision is stable enough to judge hierarchy.
