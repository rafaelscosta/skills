# Video Edit Contract

Convert taste-level direction into a decision contract an editor can optimize against.

## Required contract

Capture the smallest complete set of: `objective`, `audience`, `format`, `aspect_ratio`, duration or range when relevant, `primary_experience`, `primary_success_condition`, source boundary, `must_preserve`, `must_avoid`, delivery requirements, `risk_level`, and unresolved authority conflicts.

## Procedure

1. Extract explicit requirements from the brief.
2. Separate objective from preferred tactic or aesthetic suggestion.
3. Infer harmless defaults only when they cannot change acceptance.
4. Surface conflicts that would produce materially different edits.
5. Convert subjective language into an operational viewer or business condition where possible.
6. Preserve uncertainty rather than inventing metrics, source facts, deadlines, or platform rules.
7. Return a compact contract suitable for downstream routing.

## Decision rules

“Make it dynamic” and “make it cinematic” are not objectives. Bind the comprehension, emotion, urgency, proof, brand, retention, or response outcome they are meant to create. Platform norms are profiles, not universal laws. If persuasion conflicts with source truth, source truth wins.

Do not choose takes, write the final story, redesign graphics, or perform finishing work. This skill outputs the governing contract and material conflicts only.
