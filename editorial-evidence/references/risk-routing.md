# Risk routing

Use epistemic risk to decide how much verification work a claim deserves.

## Standard lane

Use for stable background facts and ordinary non-controversial claims. Bind at least one credible source with a precise locator and verify that it supports the exact wording.

## Heightened lane

Use when any of these applies: quantitative claim central to the argument, time-sensitive fact, important quotation, meaningful comparison, disputed topic, material estimate, or high downside from being wrong.

Add an explicit contradiction search and prefer independent evidence when practical. Preserve uncertainty, denominator, period, and comparison basis.

## Adversarial lane

Use for load-bearing causal claims, materially contested claims, claims with serious legal/medical/financial/public consequences, or claims where credible sources disagree on the mechanism.

Require competing explanations or falsifiers, active search for disconfirming evidence, and an explicit adjudication note. When available and proportionate, route the investigation through `brennerbot-with-ntm` or an equivalent hypothesis/falsifier workflow.

## Routing principle

Do not make every claim expensive. Increase rigor because the consequence and contestability increased, not because more process looks safer.
